GOOD SOUNDS!

As someone who grew up exposed to music through playing multiple instruments, singing, and having musical family members, I've always been surrounded by music. It's been an outlet for me to de-stress through picking up a guitar to putting a playlist on shuffle. I have no specific ranking of these albums, these are just some I like! I listen to all different kinds of genres so a lot of these artists are scattered all over the place. Some of them I've seen live, some of them I haven't, but they're all great artists!
